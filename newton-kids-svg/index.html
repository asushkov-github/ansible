Newton's Laws for Kids

Play with science! Push, pull, and zoom to learn how things move.

Law 1: Inertia

Things like to keep doing what they're doing! A still ball stays still. A moving ball keeps moving (unless something slows it down).

Law 2: Force = mass × acceleration

Push the same and the lighter cart zooms faster! Change the force or the masses to see what happens.

Law 3: Action and Reaction

When two friends push each other, they fly apart! The pushes are equal and opposite. Change their masses to see who moves more.
